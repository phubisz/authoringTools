# Parta.io

## Overview

Parta.io is a **productivity-first, collaboration-native** eLearning authoring platform. It functions as a team workspace combining course design, media hosting, project management, and branding governance in a single cloud environment. Built from the ground up for multi-author workflows — a direction competitors like [[tools/articulate-360|Articulate]] reportedly took over a decade to reach. Notably, Parta.io offers **native Markdown export** (Team tier) and full digital asset management with omnichannel publishing (Enterprise) — making it the only cloud tool in this comparison with a purpose-built course→Markdown pipeline for RAG/LLM workflows.

## Application

- **Ideal for**: L&D teams that co-author courses, organizations empowering SMEs to contribute content
- **Use cases**: Team-based course development, brand-consistent content at scale, project-managed learning programs
- **Not ideal for**: Solo authors who need deep assessment logic, organizations needing SCORM 1.2 legacy support

## Pricing

| Plan | Price | Key Exports & Features |
|------|-------|----------------------|
| Solo | $65/mo or $600/yr | SCORM, PDF, HTML export; 150+ layouts; 3 brand themes; AI authoring (5 courses) |
| Team | Custom | All Solo + **Markdown export**; AI translation; task management; Skilljar LMS integration; 10 AI courses/user |
| Enterprise | Custom | All Team + Digital asset management; **Omnichannel Publishing** (incl. Dynamic SCORM); SAML SSO; Phrase TMS; **AI model integration via APIs** (BYOA); 20 AI courses/user |

> The "Creator" license (~$25–$50/mo) is a monthly add-on seat that Pro/Team license holders can provision for non-L&D SME contributors — no annual commitment required. Solo pricing is the base author plan.

## Criteria Ratings

### Collaboration Options ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (5/5)
Best-in-class collaborative authoring. Multiple authors edit simultaneously in a Google Docs / Figma-style real-time environment. Built-in task assignment, Kanban boards, deadlines, and comment threads. This is a native capability, not an add-on — a key differentiator vs. [[tools/articulate-360|Articulate]] and [[tools/ispring-suite|iSpring Suite]]. See [[criteria/collaboration-options]].

### LMS Publishing Options ⭐⭐⭐ (3/5)
Supports standard SCORM and xAPI export. Less documented than enterprise tools like [[tools/articulate-360|Articulate 360]] or [[tools/dominknow|dominKnow]] regarding AICC/cmi5 support. LMS publishing is functional but not the platform's primary differentiator. See [[criteria/lms-publishing]].

### Customizable Interactions ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (4/5)
No-code layout editor with custom CSS block support gives designers significant flexibility. Wide variety of off-the-shelf templates plus the ability to build custom layouts. Falls short of [[tools/lectora|Lectora]] or [[tools/articulate-360|Storyline]] for complex branching, but well above basic tools. See [[criteria/customizable-interactions]].

### Learning Curve / Usability ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (4/5)
Clean, modern interface with Figma-influenced UX. L&D professionals find it intuitive; SMEs with no design background can contribute effectively using templates. Slightly steeper than [[tools/mindsmith|Mindsmith]] due to broader feature set. See [[criteria/learning-curve-usability]].

### Prebuilt Templates ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (4/5)
Strong library of off-the-shelf templates. Central "brand packages" (colors, typography, layouts) can be applied across all projects with one click and swapped instantly — powerful for organizations maintaining multiple brand identities. See [[criteria/prebuilt-templates]].

### Mobile / Multi-Device Compatibility ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (4/5)
Cloud-native, HTML5 output. Responsive design. No indication of specific mobile-first design emphasis, but standard cross-device compatibility is solid. See [[criteria/mobile-multidevice]].

### Multilingual and Localization Scalability ⭐⭐⭐ (3/5)
Not a primary focus of Parta.io's positioning. Translation and localization features appear limited compared to [[tools/elucidat|Elucidat]] or [[tools/easygenerator|Easygenerator]] (which support 75+ languages). Best suited for single-language or manually-translated workflows. See [[criteria/multilingual-localization]].

### LLM Ingestion Readiness ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (4/5)
Unique "bring-your-own AI" model: integrate any AI provider already in use by the organization at no extra cost, switch between providers, and toggle AI features on/off. This is forward-thinking for enterprises with existing AI governance policies. See [[criteria/llm-ingestion-readiness]].

### Advanced Logic and Assessment Capabilities ⭐⭐⭐ (3/5)
Supports standard assessment types. No evidence of advanced branching logic, variable tracking, or simulation-style interactions comparable to [[tools/articulate-360|Storyline]] or [[tools/lectora|Lectora]]. Adequate for knowledge checks and standard quizzes. See [[criteria/advanced-logic-assessment]].

### AI Features ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (4/5)
Flexible BYOA (Bring Your Own AI) architecture stands out. Rather than a proprietary AI model, Parta.io lets organizations plug in their preferred provider (OpenAI, Anthropic, etc.). AI features can be switched on/off per organizational policy — important for regulated industries. See [[criteria/ai-features]].

## User Feedback & Reviews

**Pros:**
- Real-time collaboration is genuinely best-in-class in this space
- Actively listens to user feedback; fast iteration cycle
- Brand management (central brand packages) is a standout for multi-brand orgs
- Affordable pricing with flexible Creator licenses for SMEs
- BYOA approach gives organizations AI governance flexibility

**Cons:**
- Multilingual/localization capabilities less developed
- Advanced logic/assessment less powerful than Storyline or Lectora
- Smaller ecosystem and community vs. Articulate
- Less proven at very large enterprise scale
