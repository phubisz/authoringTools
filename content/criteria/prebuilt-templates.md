# Prebuilt Templates

## Definition

Prebuilt Templates covers the **availability, quality, and breadth of ready-to-use course templates, interaction patterns, and media assets** that allow authors to produce professional-quality content without designing from scratch. This includes stock media libraries, branded template packages, and AI-generated structural templates.

**Why it matters**: Design from scratch is slow and requires visual design skills many IDs lack. Strong template libraries accelerate development, ensure visual consistency, and lower the skill bar for creating polished content.

## Key Insight

> **Articulate 360 wins by an order of magnitude**: 13 million stock assets (images, videos, icons, characters) available inside the authoring tool, plus the Articulate Marketplace and E-Learning Heroes template community. No other tool in this comparison approaches this volume.
>
> **Mindsmith reframes the template concept**: instead of choosing a template, the author describes the course and AI generates a bespoke structural scaffold. This is arguably more useful than a fixed template library because the output is tailored to the specific content rather than constrained by pre-designed patterns.
>
> **Parta.io's brand packages** are a distinct template innovation: centrally-managed brand configurations (colors, fonts, layouts) that propagate across all projects with one click — ideal for organizations managing consistent brand identity at scale.
