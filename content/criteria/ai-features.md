# AI Features

## Definition

AI Features covers the **breadth and depth of artificial intelligence capabilities integrated into the authoring tool** — including generative content creation, AI-assisted design, automated translation, intelligent assessment, and AI-powered workflows. This criterion distinguishes tools where AI is a foundational architecture from tools where it is a supplementary feature layer.

**Why it matters**: AI features directly impact authoring speed and quality. Tools with deep AI integration can reduce course creation time by 5–12x. As organizations build LLM-driven workflows, the AI architecture of authoring tools becomes a strategic selection criterion.

## Tool Commentary

### Mindsmith
The only tool in this comparison where AI is the foundational architecture. Creates lessons from prompts, generates narration, produces assessments, and grades short-answer responses with AI. Learning designers report 12x speed improvement. Uniquely offers **AI short-answer grading** — no other tool in this comparison matches this capability.

### Parta.io
The BYOA model is unique: organizations plug in their chosen AI provider (OpenAI, Anthropic, etc.) and can switch providers or disable AI entirely. This is architecturally sophisticated — especially relevant for regulated industries with AI governance requirements.

### Articulate 360
AI Assistant generates outlines, lesson content, quiz questions, and alt text. Deeply integrated into Rise 360 and Storyline 360 workflows. Strong practical AI features with the backing of the market leader's resources. "9x faster" speed claim reflects meaningful authoring acceleration.

### Easygenerator
EasyAI's document-to-course pipeline is practically impactful: organizations with large existing content repositories (policies, manuals, PDFs) can convert them to courses at scale. Combined with 75+ language translation, this is a strong AI stack for enterprise knowledge conversion.

### Elucidat
Learning Accelerator uses AI to recommend content structures based on stated learning objectives — effectively AI-guided instructional design. Combined with 75-language auto-translation, AI serves as a scalability multiplier for global content production.
